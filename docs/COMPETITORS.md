## The market splits into two models

- **Model A — SaaS tool:** the coach brings their own clients, keeps their brand,
  pays a subscription. *(Trainerize, TrueCoach, Everfit, FitBudd, Kahunas.)*
- **Model B — Marketplace/community:** the platform owns the clients and assigns
  them to coaches, taking a cut. *(FITTR.)*

We are **neither, exactly** — see "Our wedge" below.

## How FITTR works (the big Indian player)

- Started 2014 as a **WhatsApp group** → 500k-member Facebook community → app.
  **Community first, software last.**
- **Freemium funnel:** free plans + community pull you in → nudge to paid coaching
  (~₹6,000/3 months).
- **Transformation challenges** = the viral loop: users post before/after → applause
  → more people join → more before/afters. Free compounding marketing.
- **INFS** (their certification arm) manufactures coach supply and earns money.
- ~5.8M downloads, ~250k MAU, 400k+ transformations, 600–700 coaches earning
  ₹50k–3L/mo.
- **The model: FITTR owns the customer; coaches are supply.** If a coach leaves,
  the clients stay with FITTR. The coach builds FITTR's brand, not their own.

**Why this is our opening, not our competitor:** an influencer who *already has an
audience* would never give FITTR their brand and a cut of demand they created
themselves. FITTR can't serve the independent influencer without cannibalizing its
own marketplace.

## What coaches complain about (validated demand + open gaps)

1. **"Clients stop logging after 2 weeks → I'm back in WhatsApp & spreadsheets."**
   The #1 problem. Nobody has solved it.
2. Every tool's **coach mobile app is the weakest piece** — all desktop-first.
3. **Add-on fees** — the sticker price is a lie.
4. **4–8 hours to learn.** Onboarding a coach eats a week.

## Steal this — best lessons per tool

| Tool | Superpower | What we take |
|---|---|---|
| TrueCoach | Chat + workout comments = the accountability engine; 1,200+ demo videos | Chat/check-ins are core; coaches can drop demo clips |
| Everfit | Best UI + automation ("Autoflow") | Automate the repetitive coach grind; clean UI |
| Trainerize | Complete loop (onboarding → billing → wearables) | Close the whole loop; kill WhatsApp + Sheets + GPay |
| FitBudd / Kahunas | White-label + flat price + 0% | Coach's brand; one predictable price; don't nickel-and-dime |
| **All** | Universal failure: clients quit logging; weak coach app | **Win exactly there** — a client app people keep using, coach-experience phone-first |
| FITTR | Transformation-challenge viral loop | Give coaches a "run a challenge" tool pointed at *their* Instagram |

## Our wedge

> A **creator-first platform** for coaches who **already have their own demand**,
> letting them keep **their brand, clients, and money** — **India-first**
> (INR, UPI, Razorpay), **mobile-first**, **everything included at one flat price**,
> with **a client app people actually keep using** (our consumer-side head start).

FITTR can't chase it (would cannibalize their marketplace). The Western tools can't
price for the Indian influencer charging ₹2,000/month or pay out over UPI. That gap
is the company.
